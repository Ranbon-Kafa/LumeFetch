## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: production square application icon for LumeFetch, an open-source desktop media downloader.
Primary request: an original minimal geometric symbol merging a bold capital L with a downward download arrow. A thick mint L forms a receiving corner/tray; a compact downward arrow is neatly nested in its upper-right negative space. Make it one balanced memorable mark.
Style/medium: clean flat vector-like logo rendered as a crisp bitmap, strict geometry, subtly rounded joins, high contrast.
Composition: single centered mint symbol occupying about 65% of square canvas, navy rounded-square app tile fills canvas nearly edge-to-edge, generous even internal padding. Genuinely transparent outside rounded tile corners.
Palette: solid mint #6EE7B7 on deep navy #0C121B.
Constraints: symbol only, no words, no small letters, no mockup, no perspective, no shadows, no glow, no gradients, no extra panels, no watermark. Strong readable silhouette at 32px. Do not include platform logos.
